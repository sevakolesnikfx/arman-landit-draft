What it is

The main means-tested payment. 2026/27 monthly standard allowances: single under 25 £338.58, single 25+ £424.90, couple both under 25 £528.34, couple one 25+ £666.97. Child element £303.94 per child (+£47.94 first child born before 6 April 2017); two-child limit abolished April 2026. Childcare element 85% up to £1,071.09 / £1,836.16. Work allowances £427 / £710, 55p taper. Carer element £209.34. LCWRA health element £217.26 for new claims (pre-April 2026 claims keep £428.90). Savings under £16,000. First payment about 5 weeks; advances from day 1.

About the amount: £424.90/month single 25+ (2026/27, verified); child element £303.94/child, two-child limit abolished April 2026; childcare 85% to £1,071.09; LCWRA £217.26 for new claims.

Improve your chances
Claim the DAY your entitlement starts: there is no general backdating and every day of delay is money gone.
Ask for an advance from day one if the 5-week wait is unmanageable: repayable over 24 months.
Report childcare costs in the assessment period you actually paid them, or you lose the 85% reimbursement.
The two-child limit was abolished in April 2026: every child counts now.

Most common mistake

Savings must be under £16,000. Refugees should claim on the day status is granted, not when the paperwork settles.
