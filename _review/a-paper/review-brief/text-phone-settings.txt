Help now

In immediate danger, call 999.

Helplines like Victim Support, Rape Crisis and the domestic abuse lines are independent charities. They will not ask about your immigration status, and helping you does not depend on going to the police. Being a victim of crime is never illegal, whatever your status.

Cannot speak safely

On a mobile, stay on the line and press 55 when prompted: the call transfers to police. On a landline stay silent; it stays connected about 45 seconds after hanging up.
