What it is

Composition-based, not means-tested, so NRPF-safe: full-time student exemption (Class N, all-student household pays nothing, and it covers the student's non-working non-British spouse or dependant), 25% single person discount, 50% all-disregarded. Never confuse with Council Tax Reduction, which is a public fund.

Do you qualify?
Full-time course, 1 year+ and 21+ hours a week
All occupants students or disregarded
Sole or main residence

Listed for: Student visa, Graduate visa

Improve your chances
The exemption also covers the non-British spouse or dependant of a full-time student who cannot work or claim under their visa: include them and send their share code.
Always ask for backdating. Councils do backdate with evidence, and that can be a year or more of bills.
You need a course of 1 year or more, and 21+ study hours a week.
Ask for backdating to course or tenancy start
Most common mistake

Do not confuse this with Council Tax Reduction, which is a public fund and blocked under NRPF. This exemption is based on who lives in the property, not income, so it is NRPF-safe.

Steps

0 of 5 steps done. Tap a step to tick it off.

1
Get a council tax student certificate from your university: it is free and usually instant online.
2
Find your council's council tax discount page.
3
Apply for the Class N student exemption. If everyone in the household is a student, the bill is zero.
4
Upload the certificate and your tenancy.
5
Ask for it to be backdated to your course or tenancy start date.
