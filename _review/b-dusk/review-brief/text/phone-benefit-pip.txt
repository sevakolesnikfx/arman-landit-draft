What it is

Disability extra-costs benefit, not means-tested, work allowed. 2026/27 weekly: daily living £76.70 / £114.60, mobility £30.30 / £80.00 (max £194.60/week). The 2025 four-point rule was withdrawn; no eligibility change is law as of August 2026 (Timms review reports autumn 2026). Past presence: 104 of 156 weeks (Refugee, Ukraine exempt). Scotland: ADP instead.

Do you qualify?
Daily living or mobility needs 3 months back, 9 forward
104 of last 156 weeks in GB (refugee/Ukraine exempt)
Points thresholds: 8 standard, 12 enhanced

Listed for: Refugee or humanitarian protection, Ukraine scheme visa, EU Settlement Scheme, settled status, EU Settlement Scheme, pre-settled status, Indefinite leave to remain

Improve your chances
Give real, specific examples per descriptor: "I dropped a full pan twice last month" beats "I struggle with cooking".
Use the 50% rule: if you cannot do something safely, repeatedly and in reasonable time on more than half of days, you cannot do it. Say that explicitly.
Keep a 7-day diary before you write the form and quote from it.
New claim award rate is 37%, but most refusals that are challenged are overturned at appeal: do not stop at the first no.
Pre-settled status holders CAN claim disability benefits: there is no right-to-reside test for these.
Real examples per descriptor, bad-day variability, 7-day diary, attach professional evidence, appeal refusals
Most common mistake

Describing your best day. Assessors record what you say literally, and a good day on paper becomes a refusal.

Before you start, gather

What you need

A 7-day symptom diary
Medical evidence: GP letters, consultant reports, prescriptions
A list of every difficulty with daily living and getting around
Details of aids and adaptations you use

Track this to keep the checklist in your sidebar.

Steps

0 of 6 steps done. Tap a step to tick it off.

1
Ring the PIP claim line to start the claim: the date you ring is your claim date.
2
Complete the "How your disability affects you" form. Take your time: this is the whole claim.
3
For each activity, describe a bad day, how long tasks take, and what happens afterwards.
4
Attach professional evidence.
5
Attend the assessment if you are asked to.
6
If refused, request a mandatory reconsideration, then appeal.
