What it is

Free A-level-equivalent qualification from the approved list: free for any 19-23 year old without a full Level 3, and for any adult unemployed or earning under £26,800 (2026/27). Standard adult skills residency: refugees, HP, ILR, Ukraine immediate; most visa holders and EUSS after 3 years residence.

Do you qualify?

The catalog lists no extra rules. Check the official page.

Improve your chances
There is a route in, but it depends on your circumstances: ask which route applies to you before assuming no.
If you are told no at the counter, ask for the reason in writing. Front-line refusals are often wrong about immigration status.
