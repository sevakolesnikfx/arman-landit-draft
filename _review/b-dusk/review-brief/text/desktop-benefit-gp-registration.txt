What it is

Free primary care and the gateway to all NHS services. No ID, no proof of address, no immigration status required (NHS policy). Homeless patients can use the surgery's address.

Do you qualify?
Live in or near the practice area
No ID, proof of address or immigration status required (NHS policy)
Improve your chances
If you are turned away, ask for the refusal in writing with the reason. Practices can only refuse if the list is closed or you are outside the area.
Register before you are ill: it is the gateway to nearly everything else in the NHS.
If refused, ask for the refusal in writing
Most common mistake

Accepting "we need ID and proof of address" as final. It is not a legal requirement.
