What can I claim?

On a Student visa in England, you may be able to get 80 schemes and services. 8 of them have an amount. 13 others are not open to you because of your visa condition.

Here are 3 to look at first. Council Tax student exemption (Class N): £1,800 (typical annual saving). British Gas Energy Trust grants: £1,700 (up to, one-off). University hardship fund: £1,400 (typical amount).

All the amounts together come to £8,464. That is the potential, if all approved. See every scheme in Benefits.
