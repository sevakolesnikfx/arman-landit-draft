Council Tax student exemption (Class N)
Council tax as a student

I did not know students could stop paying council tax. I got a student certificate from my university online. I sent it to the council with my tenancy. I also asked them to backdate it to my course start.

University hardship fund
Hardship fund: write a clear budget

My work hours were cut, so I applied to my university hardship fund. I wrote a simple budget: money in, money out. I added three months of bank statements. The student union adviser read my form first, and that helped.

16-25 Railcard
Railcard with a passport from home

I thought I needed a UK ID for the railcard. You do not. I used my own passport and a photo from my phone. Now I add the card to my ticket app before I pay.

GP registration
Registering with a GP with no proof of address

I had just moved and had no letters with my address. At first the receptionist said I needed proof. I said NHS policy lets you register without it. They registered me the same week.

NHS Low Income Scheme (HC2/HC3)
Help with prescriptions and the dentist

I filled in the NHS low income form online. I listed my rent and council tax too, because the guide said it matters. My certificate took a few weeks. I waited for it before I booked the dentist.

Emergency tax on my first payslip

My first payslip had a tax code that ended in W1. I checked GOV.UK and it was an emergency code. I sorted it in my personal tax account. I did not use a refund company.
